# How Servant's Research Studio uses Civic Research Studio

- **Build a research study** in Servant makes a study plan on the researcher's
  device and can save a *working draft* (study and plan) to open with Import
  JSON in Civic Research Studio 0.3.24's builder. It's a draft to keep
  refining, not a finished or reviewed plan. The researcher writes the welcome
  and consent text in Civic Research Studio.
- A study can use a prototype from Servant's Design Studio. Prototype previews
  are viewable by anyone with their link, so they hold made-up data only.
- What the research found goes to the prototype agent only in general terms:
  no names, quotes, links, record IDs, dates or numbers. Research Hub evidence
  stays with the Researcher Agent; only guidance the researcher reviews and
  approves goes to the prototype agent.
- **Write a study readout** reads the study file and the Qualtrics exports on
  the researcher's device and computes results with Civic Research Studio's own
  code: rates over everyone assigned with 95% ranges, differences from design
  A, median times, ratings, and comments the researcher codes into themes. It
  hides designs and theme counts under the small-group minimums from what's
  shared, blocks quotes with personal details, and blocks overclaiming words
  ("validated", "proven", "winner", "significant").

When helping with a readout or synthesis, follow the same rules: no winners,
cautious wording, ranges with every rate, and no personal details in quotes.
