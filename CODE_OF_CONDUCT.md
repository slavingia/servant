# Code of conduct

Be kind, be direct, assume good faith. This is a small project run by people with day jobs.

## We expect

- Respectful discussion, including when you disagree
- Focus on the work, not the person
- Credit for other people's ideas and fixes

## We do not accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Sharing someone's private information
- Sexual or discriminatory language or images
- Spam, or using the project to promote unrelated things

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and commits, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** on the item, or open a private security advisory and say it is a conduct report.
