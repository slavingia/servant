# Code of conduct

Be kind, be direct, assume good faith. This is a small project run by people with day jobs.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Servant to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## We expect

- Respectful discussion, including when you disagree
- Focus on the work, not the person
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Credit for other people's ideas and fixes

## We do not accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, email or screenshots of their conversations, without their permission
- Sexual or discriminatory language or images
- Spam, or using the project to promote unrelated things

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commits) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainers enforce this code. They may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests and commits, and warn, or temporarily or permanently block, people who break it.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** on the item, or report it privately to the maintainers through **Security > Advisories > Report a vulnerability** on this repository and say in the title that it is a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential, and we will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.
