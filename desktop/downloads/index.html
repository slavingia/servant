Install and sign in

  1. 1

    Install Servant on your Mac

    Download the version for your Mac, open the disk image, and drag Servant to Applications. Open Servant to see your sign-in code.

  2. 2

    Update your phone app

    Install Servant build 25 or later through your existing TestFlight, Firebase App Distribution, or Google Play testing access. Sign in on your phone first.

  3. 3

    Scan and confirm

    On your phone, open You → Link a computer. Scan the QR code on your Mac, check that the numbers match, and approve sign-in.

Only approve a Mac you’re signing in to. If the code expires, generate a new one in the Mac app. If “Link a computer” is missing, check your phone’s build number and update the app.
