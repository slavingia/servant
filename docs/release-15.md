Servant 1.0.0 (15)

What's new
- Search channels, people, messages, and agent chat titles from a fixed search bar. On web, use Cmd/Ctrl+K.
- A consistent design system throughout the app, with clearer navigation, readable type, and quieter archived channels.
- Online and last-seen status, plus read receipts in direct messages, channels, and threads.
- Photo and file attachments with inline previews, and easier message reactions and text selection.
- Encrypted app-to-server connections and improved recovery after reconnecting.

What to test
- Find a channel, teammate, or older message with search. Message results should open and highlight the match.
- Archive and restore a channel; archived conversations stay out of the active list.
- Send a photo or file, react to a message, and reply in a thread.
- Check online/last-seen status and read receipts between two devices.
- Leave the app, return, and confirm your conversations and drafts recover.

Existing installations may ask you to reconnect securely. Ask the server owner
for a fresh invite for your existing account to keep your conversations. A
general team invite creates a new account.
