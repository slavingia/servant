Messaging for public servants, with an agent you can ask

Channels, direct messages and threads, on a hub your agency runs. Ask the agent in any channel and everyone there sees the answer.

No consumer chat service in the middle. Invites only.

Internal pilot · iOS and Android
ONE

Getting in

Someone already on the hub sends you an invite. Scan it or paste the link, and you're in. No accounts, no passwords.

TWO

Channels and threads

Talk with your team in the open. Reply in a thread to keep the channel readable. New messages show up live.

THREE

The agent

Type @agent in a channel and it answers in a thread, so everyone learns from it. Or chat with it privately in the Agent tab.

FOUR

Direct messages

One person or a small group, away from the channels. Add the agent by typing @agent.

How it works

  1. The appOn iPhone and Android. Each phone gets its own key
  2. The hubA small server your agency runs. It decides who's in and what they can see
  3. The agentRuns next to the hub. Its keys never leave that computer
  4. The peopleInvites only. Members get a limited agent with no access to files or accounts
