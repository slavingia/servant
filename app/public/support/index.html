Servant support

For help with the app, accessibility, or a bug, ask the administrator of your team’s server. Include your device, app version and what happened. Do not include passwords, invite links or private messages.

Joining your team

Your team runs its own server and sends you an invite. Open Servant, choose I'm new here, paste the invite link and tap Continue. Read the terms, check the three boxes, tap Agree and join, then enter your name.

If your name is already taken, add an initial. If you already belong to this team, link your new device from You → Link a computer on a device where you are signed in.

Server access

Ask your team's server administrator about invitations, membership, server availability and access to team content.

Deleting your account or data

Contact the administrator of the server you joined to request deletion of your account and data. If you cannot reach the administrator, ask the person who invited you for their contact details. Signing out removes that device's access; it does not delete your server account or messages.
