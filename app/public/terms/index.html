Version 2026-11-11 · Updated November 11, 2026

Servant is independent, open-source team messaging software. Each team runs its own server. Joining gives you access to that team's workspace; it does not create an account with a hosted Servant service.

1. Use Servant responsibly

Use Servant for work, follow your organisation's rules and applicable law, and keep your invite and device secure. Only join servers you have permission to access. Do not harass people, share unlawful content, or interfere with other people's access.

2. Check the agent's work

The agent can be wrong. Check what it tells you and does before you act on it. You are responsible for what you send or do with its help, including any actions you approve. Your team's chosen AI provider may process the information you give the agent.

3. Keep sensitive information out

Do not share personal, confidential or other sensitive information. Your team's server has not been reviewed for it. That includes anything you would not want a server administrator to read. Servant does not claim government approval or a security certification.

Your content and your team's server

Your messages, files and profile are stored on your team's server, whose administrators can access them. Your team controls membership, retention and access to its workspace, and may remove access. Only share content you have permission to share. See the privacy policy for how data is handled.

Leaving and getting help

You can stop using Servant and sign out at any time. Signing out removes a device's access, not your messages or account on the server. Ask your server administrator to delete your account or data, or contact the administrator of your team’s server for help.

Software and availability

Servant's source code is available under CC0. The software is provided as is. Your team operates the server and is responsible for its availability and backups. These workspace commitments do not replace the software's open-source licence or any applicable app-store licence.
