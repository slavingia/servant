Filled Material Symbols for Android and web tab icons.

Rendered as white transparent masks from MaterialSymbols_400Regular_Filled.ttf
in @expo-google-fonts/material-symbols (already included by expo-symbols).
The native tab bar applies selected/unselected tint. Logical size is 24 points;
@2x and @3x files preserve detail on higher-density displays. iOS uses SF Symbols.

Glyphs: home U+E88A; forum U+E0BF; auto_awesome U+E65F;
groups U+F233; account_circle U+E853.
